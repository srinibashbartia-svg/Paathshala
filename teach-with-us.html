The Educator’s Paybook Summary

Key rules and protections distilled from our 2026 transparency charter.

Chapter 1: The ₹290/class Baseline Formula

Every standard 1-on-1 session is compensated at ₹290 net to your bank. As you complete retention milestones (40+ sessions), you unlock automatic +₹35/class boosts, and advanced competitive coaching batches pay up to ₹450–₹550 per session.

Chapter 2: The Monthly Auto-Deposit Engine

All sessions completed during the month are tallied automatically. Payment initiates every single month directly to your verified UPI ID or IMPS bank account. No minimum withdrawal thresholds.

Chapter 3: The 24-Hour Student Cancellation Shield

If a parent reschedules or cancels a class under 24 hours before start time, you receive 100% of the class payout. You reserved your time, and we value that commitment.

Chapter 4: Pedagogical Autonomy Charter

You have complete teaching freedom without rigid corporate scripts. You have full liberty to adjust pacing, introduce creative analogies, and address where each student specifically struggles.
